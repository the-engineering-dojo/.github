# 🥋 The Engineering Dojo

A shared place to **practice engineering, not just ship code**.

We learn by discussing trade-offs, designing experiments, breaking assumptions, reviewing each other's work, and documenting what changed our minds.

> 💡 **Learning-first, human-first.** AI may support research and reviews, but understanding must be demonstrated by the people contributing.

## ✨ How we work

**Understand → Hypothesize → Experiment → Measure → Review → Explain.**

No arbitrary microservices, no patterns for their own sake, and no unreviewed AI-generated solutions.
