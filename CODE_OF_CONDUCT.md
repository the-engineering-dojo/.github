# 💛 Code of Conduct

We want The Engineering Dojo to be welcoming to developers with different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.

**Be respectful:** discuss ideas and evidence, not someone's worth or intelligence.  
**Be constructive:** offer examples, ask questions, and be open to changing your mind.  
**Be inclusive:** avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or unwanted attention.  
**Be patient:** people are participating in their free time and may be learning unfamiliar topics.

Maintainers may moderate issues and contributions that violate these principles. If you need to report a sensitive concern, reach an organization owner privately through an available GitHub contact channel rather than posting personal information in a public issue.

This is a short project-specific conduct guide, not a claim of affiliation with other organizations.
